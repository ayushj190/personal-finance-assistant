# Privacy Policy & Data Protection

**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026  
**Application:** Personal Finance Assistant (PFA)  
**Contact / Data Protection Officer:** ayush@joshi.contact  

## 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy
Personal Finance Assistant is architected as a **local-first, self-hosted** application. Our foundational principle is data sovereignty: your financial data belongs to you and should never leave your personal computer without explicit consent. 

This policy is designed to comply with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and the **EU Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act)**.

## 2. Data We Process & Storage Location
To provide financial tracking, budgeting, and AI assistance, the application processes:
- **Account details:** Account identifiers, IBANs, account names, and currencies.
- **Transactions:** Dates, descriptions, counterparties, and transaction amounts.
- **Portfolios:** Investment holdings, tickers, and cost bases.
- **Risk Profiles:** Your answers to investment risk questionnaires and resulting scores.

### **100% Local Storage Guarantee**
All the above data, along with categorization rules and authentication tokens, is stored **exclusively** on your local machine.
- **Database:** Stored in a local SQLite file (`data/finance.db`).
- **Secrets:** API keys and credentials are encrypted using AES (Fernet) in `data/vault.bin`, secured by Windows Credential Manager.
- **No Telemetry:** We do not track your usage, sell your data, or send analytics to third-party servers.

## 3. Third-Party Integrations & Consent
The application interfaces with third parties only when explicitly configured and initiated by you:
1. **Enable Banking Oy (AISP):** Used to sync bank data via Open Banking (PSD2). This requires your explicit Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) directly with your bank. You may revoke consent at any time.
2. **Yahoo Finance (`yfinance`):** Used to fetch public market data for investment tickers. No personal data is transmitted.
3. **Brave Search API (Optional):** If enabled, public merchant names (e.g., "MCDONALDS AMSTERDAM") are queried to improve categorization. **No transaction amounts, balances, or IBANs are ever transmitted.**
4. **TradingView Widget:** Used to display live market prices for direct holdings. This is a client-side embedded widget. Your browser directly connects to TradingView servers, which means your IP address and the specific ticker symbols requested are visible to TradingView in accordance with their privacy policy.

## 4. EU AI Act Compliance (Artificial Intelligence Processing)
The application utilizes an **Offline AI Copilot** (via Ollama) to analyze your finances, categorize transactions, and assess risk tolerance.
- **Local Processing:** The LLM runs locally. Your financial data is not sent to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or any cloud AI provider.
- **Transparency:** The AI is used to translate natural language into SQL/Python and generate visualizations. The exact SQL or Python code generated by the AI is available for you to inspect in the UI.
- **No Automated Legal/Financial Decisions:** The AI provides informational summaries, drift calculations, and categorization suggestions. It does **not** make automated decisions that produce legal effects or execute financial trades (Article 5 & Annex III of the AI Act). All actions (e.g., rebalancing, submitting tax reports) require human intervention and oversight.
- **Risk Profiling:** The application computes a risk profile score based strictly on user-provided questionnaire answers. This is used solely to contextualize your portfolio drift locally.

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR)
Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have total, unmediated control over your data:
- **Right to Access & Portability:** You can export your data by copying the SQLite database (`data/finance.db`).
- **Right to Rectification:** You can manually edit transactions and account balances in the app.
- **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You can permanently delete all your personal data by deleting the application folder and clearing your browser's local storage. There are no remote backups.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You can revoke Open Banking consent via your bank's portal or by deleting the connection in the app settings.

For any inquiries regarding data protection, please contact: `ayush@joshi.contact`.
